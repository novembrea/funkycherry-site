Privacy Policy

Prismfall · last updated 18 October 2026

Prismfall is a game by funkycherry ("we"). This policy explains what information the game collects and why. In short: no account, no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and nothing that identifies you by name.

What we collect

The game uses Google Firebase to understand how it is played and to fix crashes:

  • Gameplay and usage data (Firebase Analytics): events such as a run starting and ending, the wave reached, the score, upgrades picked, settings changed and frame-rate statistics, together with a random app-instance identifier, your device model, iOS version, language and approximate country (derived from your IP address, which is not stored).
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): when the game crashes, a report with the technical state of the app, your device model and iOS version, and the same random identifier.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or the advertising identifier (IDFA), and we do not use the data for advertising.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center. Apple handles your Game Center profile and scores under Apple's privacy policy; we only see the scores and achievements Game Center shows to any player.

How we use it

Only to improve the game: balancing difficulty, finding bugs and crashes, and checking performance. We do not sell or share the data with anyone else. Google processes it for us as a service provider under the Firebase privacy and security terms.

How long we keep it

Analytics data is kept for 14 months, crash reports for 90 days, then deleted. Your scores and settings saved on your iPhone stay there until you delete the app.

Your choices

Deleting the app stops all collection and resets the random identifier. To ask about or delete data linked to your app instance, write to support@funkycherry.games.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if ads are added in a later version), we will update this page and the date above before the change ships.
